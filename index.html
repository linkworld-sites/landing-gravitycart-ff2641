The Build

Engineered by people who build cars.

Every component of the Gravity Cart Sport is validated against automotive reliability, safety and simplicity standards — not adapted from a toy.

S

Stainless Steel Fork

An optimised steering fork absorbs shock while holding its form under load, delivering stability, sharp handling and a distinct visual signature — with integrated towing fixation.

A

Adjustable Seat

Automotive-inspired seating with a dimensioned backrest, replaceable two-part cover, and up to 400mm of combined adjustment for any rider.

B

Shimano Dual Brakes

Independent front and rear Ø180mm disc braking, cable-actuated to resist fade — legally required, market-unique performance under all conditions.

F

Unisize Aluminum Frame

A central aluminum structure that holds every critical part directly, enabling modular configuration, easy access and one frame that fits all.

W

Well-Dimensioned Wheels

Robust front and rear wheels tuned for driving dynamics — confident on descent, composed under braking, distinctive in appearance.

C

CE / ISO Compliant

Designed against road regulation and CE/ISO criteria from day one — no dangerous liquids, mono-material construction, 100% recyclable.
