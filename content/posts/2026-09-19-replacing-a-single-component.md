A question we hear from owners and dealers alike, usually after a hard landing or a season of hard use: "I bent the fork / burned through a rotor — do I need to send back the whole cart?"

No. The GC-SPORT-01 was engineered as a serviceable machine, not a sealed unit, and every wear or crash-prone assembly on it is sold as a standalone, certified part.

## What's actually replaceable

- **Stainless Steel Fork (GC-FRK-100)** — 304 stainless, 42mm rake, tool-free quick-release mount. If a fork takes a direct hit in a crash, this is the part that's designed to absorb it, and the part you replace — not the chassis behind it.
- **Shimano Dual Brake Kit (GC-BRK-180)** — the hydraulic dual-disc set, 180mm rotors front and rear, ECE R100 tested. Rotors and pads wear with use like any braking system; this kit is sold complete so a replacement goes back on tested to the same standard as the original, not a mismatched aftermarket substitute.
- **Ski Assembly Kit (GC-SKI-200)** — sold independently of the fork and brake kit, this is the same certified conversion kit described in [how the chassis converts between modes](/blog/2026-09-02-gravity-powered-descent-carts), available on its own if you're re-equipping a chassis for winter or replacing a damaged ski assembly.

## Why we sell parts, not just carts

This follows directly from how the chassis itself is built: a mono-material 6061-T6 aluminum frame chosen in part because it's straightforward to repair with regionally sourced hardware, not a proprietary structure that forces a factory return. The fork, brake kit and ski assembly are the same logic applied to the wear components — each one mounts to the same certified attachment points as the original, at the same torque specs, so a replacement part restores the chassis to its original 120kg-rated certification rather than degrading it.

A cart that can only be repaired by the factory isn't a seasonal vehicle you own for years — it's a single-use product with a long warranty. We built the opposite.

## Getting the right part

If you're not sure which component failed or which kit your chassis needs, check the part number stamped near the mount point against the specification exhibits above — each part on GravityCart.com is listed under its own part number (GC-FRK-100, GC-BRK-180, GC-SKI-200) for exactly this reason.
