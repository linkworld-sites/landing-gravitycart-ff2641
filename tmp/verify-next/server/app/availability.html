Does GravityCart sell through retailers or regional dealers?
No. Every GC-SPORT-01 chassis and every replacement assembly (GC-SKI-200, GC-FRK-100, GC-BRK-180) ships direct from GravityCart through this storefront. There is no third-party retail or dealer network — this keeps the certification chain of custody intact from assembly to buyer.
Which regions is the Gravity Cart Sport certified for?
The GC-SPORT-01 chassis is documented against ECE 2006/42/EG and ISO EN 12100 / ISO EN 4210, the machinery and safety directives referenced across the EU. The Shimano Dual Brake Kit (GC-BRK-180) is separately tested to ECE R100, a UNECE braking standard recognized across the EU and other UNECE-contracting markets.
Can I order replacement parts like the fork, ski kit or brake kit on their own?
Yes. The Stainless Steel Fork (GC-FRK-100), Ski Assembly Kit (GC-SKI-200) and Shimano Dual Brake Kit (GC-BRK-180) are each sold as standalone SKUs through this storefront, independent of a full GC-SPORT-01 order.
Is availability limited by season or region?
No — the GC-SPORT-01 is a single convertible chassis with certified wheel and ski assemblies included, so the same order covers pavement, gravel and packed-snow use rather than a region- or season-specific model.
