How a Gravity-Powered Descent Cart Converts Between Wheels and Skis

2026-09-02

A gravity-powered descent cart carries no motor. Descent grade supplies the motion; the chassis, the brake and the terrain interface do the rest of the engineering work. That is the brief the GC-SPORT-01 is built to — a certified, all-season chassis engineered for two terrain configurations, not a seasonal toy built for one.

The wheel-to-ski conversion

The GC-SPORT-01 chassis mounts the terrain interface at the same certified attachment points regardless of season. For pavement, gravel and hardpack, that interface is the standard wheel assembly. For packed snow, it is the Ski Assembly Kit (GC-SKI-200): a certified ski conversion with a tool-free binding, 780mm ski length, rated to a 25% descent grade. Swapping between the two is a binding release and re-engage on the same chassis — no separate frame, no second vehicle to maintain, no re-certification per season.

The certified chassis

The GC-SPORT-01 is the flagship convertible chassis: certified wheel and ski assemblies included, rated to a 120kg load across both configurations. A chassis that is only certified in one mode is not a seasonal vehicle — it is a wheeled vehicle with an aftermarket ski attached. The GC-SPORT-01's rated load and certification hold across the conversion, which is what makes the swap a configuration change rather than a downgrade.

The Shimano dual-disc brake

Stopping is the one job on a gravity-powered vehicle that cannot be approximate. The Shimano Dual Brake Kit (GC-BRK-180) is a hydraulic dual-disc set, 180mm rotors front and rear, tested against the ECE R100 braking standard. It ships as the braking system for the GC-SPORT-01 chassis in both wheel and ski configuration — the same rotor size and test standard whichever terrain interface is mounted.

Frequently asked questions

FAQ

How does the wheel-to-ski conversion work on the Gravity Cart Sport?
The GC-SPORT-01 chassis mounts its terrain interface at the same certified attachment points year-round. Swapping to winter use means releasing the wheel assembly and engaging the Ski Assembly Kit (GC-SKI-200) — a tool-free binding, no separate frame.
What terrain and grade is the ski conversion rated for?
The GC-SKI-200 ski assembly is 780mm in length and rated to a 25% descent grade on packed snow, fitted to the GC-SPORT-01 chassis.
What braking hardware does the Gravity Cart Sport use?
The Shimano Dual Brake Kit (GC-BRK-180) is a hydraulic dual-disc set with 180mm front and rear rotors, tested against the ECE R100 braking standard.
What is the load rating of the certified chassis?
The GC-SPORT-01 chassis is rated to 120kg, with certified wheel and ski assemblies included, engineered for pavement, gravel and packed snow.
Is the Gravity Cart Sport CE and ISO compliant?
Yes. The Gravity Cart Sport is independently tested and documented against ECE 2006/42/EG, ISO EN 12100 and ISO EN 4210 — the same category of machinery and safety directives that govern the automotive and machinery industries.
