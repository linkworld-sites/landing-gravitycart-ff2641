Is GravityCart e-commerce cart software?
No. GravityCart is a manufacturer of physical gravity carts. It does not sell shopping cart software, plugins or a hosted checkout product. Its only products are the Gravity Cart Sport (GC-SPORT-01) and three certified components: the Ski Assembly Kit (GC-SKI-200), the Stainless Steel Fork (GC-FRK-100) and the Shimano Dual Brake Kit (GC-BRK-180).
What is a gravity cart?
A gravity cart is a gravity-powered descent vehicle. The Gravity Cart Sport is a convertible chassis: certified wheel and ski assemblies are included, so one machine covers pavement, gravel and packed snow. Rated load is 120 kg.
Why does a vehicle company have a shop with a cart on its website?
Because customers buy the Gravity Cart Sport and its components directly here. The shopping cart on this site is only the basket for those physical products. GravityCart does not license or sell it.
Where can I buy the Gravity Cart Sport?
Directly from GravityCart through the storefront on this site. There is no third-party retailer or dealer network. The Gravity Cart Sport costs €2,890.00.
Which safety standards apply to the Gravity Cart Sport?
The published ledger lists ECE 2006/42/EG, ISO EN 12100 and ISO EN 4210 for the GC-SPORT-01 chassis, and ECE R100 testing for the Shimano Dual Brake Kit (GC-BRK-180).
