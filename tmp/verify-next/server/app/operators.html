GRAVITYCART FOR BUSINESS

Give your guests something to talk about

The Gravity Cart Sport is built for rental and operator use as much as for private riders — one automotive-engineered chassis and Shimano dual-disc brakes that hold up to all-day fleet use.

Summer Lift Operators

Turn an existing chairlift into a summer attraction: guests ride up, then descend by cart on wheels down the same slopes used for winter sport.

Adventure Tour Operators

Package a guided cart descent alongside via ferrata, e-bike or hiking tours — a bookable, photogenic centerpiece for a half-day itinerary.

Mountain Lodges & Huts

Offer cart rides as a guest activity that keeps visitors on-site longer, backed by automotive-grade engineering built for all-day fleet use.

Holiday Parks & Campgrounds

A family-friendly activity fleet — the adjustable seat fits riders from age 12 to 99, so one machine serves the whole group.

Bike Parks & Trail Centers

Give non-bikers and families an accessible way to use the same downhill terrain, without competing for trail time with the bike fleet.

Event & Group Rentals

Corporate outings, team-building days and group races: a turnkey activity that scales from a handful of riders to a full fleet.
